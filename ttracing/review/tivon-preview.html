Before you begin

Make room for your new desk.

Check both cartons and prepare a flat, protected surface. Keep the electrical components away from water and high humidity.

  • Locate the tabletop and flippable bar in box 1.
  • Check the frame supports, extendable bars, feet and accessories.
  • Keep the supplied 4 mm and 6 mm Allen keys within reach.

Frame alignment depends on tabletop width. The guide will help you match the numbered bar marks before fitting the frame.
