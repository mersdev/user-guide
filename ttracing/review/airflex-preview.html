Before you begin

Get ready to build your Airflex.

Open the carton on a protected floor. Check that you have the original Airflex parts shown in the supplied manual.

  • Find the backrest, seatbase and left and right armrests.
  • Check the wheelbase, five castors, gas lift and full-tilt mechanism.
  • Locate the Allen key and the three sizes of M6 screws.

Fit screws loosely first. The manual calls for final tightening after all the parts are aligned.
